Privacy Policy

Last updated September 3, 2026

What This App Collects

Coloring Fish saves the fish artwork guests submit, the selected fish type, an optional fish name, and a short optional fish bio used on the aquarium display.

How It Is Used

Submissions are used to show the fish in the shared aquarium for the event. The app does not use accounts, advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or payment information.

AI Description Helper

If the description helper is enabled by the site owner, a reduced-size preview of the fish artwork, the fish type, and the optional fish name may be sent to Hugging Face to suggest a short aquarium bio. If that service is unavailable, the app creates a local fallback description.

Storage And Deletion

Fish are stored for the current event day. Old days are purged automatically by scheduled cleanup and when the app runs. Event staff can also reset the aquarium. Submitted images are marked private and not stored in shared public caches; a browser may retain temporary in-memory copies while the aquarium page is open.

Public Display

Anything submitted can appear on the aquarium screen and may be visible to people at the event. Guests should avoid entering full names or private details.

Questions

Ask the event organizer or site owner if you need a submission removed during the event.
